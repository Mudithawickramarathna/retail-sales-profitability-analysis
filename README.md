# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores retail sales and profitability using Python. The goal is to identify sales trends, understand product and customer performance, and generate business insights that can support better decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.
- Examine monthly and yearly sales and profit trends.
- Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Compare customer segments and regional performance.
- Present findings through data visualizations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Squarify
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Data quality checks and date conversion
- Key performance indicator (KPI) calculations
- Monthly and yearly trend analysis
- Category and sub-category profitability analysis
- Discount analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Regional sales and profit analysis
- Scatter plots, line charts, bar charts, pie charts, treemaps, and correlation heatmaps

## Key Findings
- **Yearly performance:** Sales and profit reached their highest levels in 2017 in the analyzed dataset.
- **Product categories:** Technology had the highest category sales and a profit margin of approximately 17.40%.
- **Furniture profitability:** Furniture had a relatively low profit margin of approximately 2.49%.
- **Discounts:** Higher discount levels were frequently associated with losses in the dataset.
- **Customer segments:** Consumer customers generated the highest total sales and profit, while Home Office had the highest segment profit margin at approximately 14.03%.
- **Regional performance:** The West region had the highest sales and a profit margin of approximately 14.94%.
- **Sub-categories:** Copiers generated the highest total profit, while Tables recorded the largest total loss.

## Business Recommendations
- Review discount policies for transactions with negative profits.
- Investigate the costs and discount patterns associated with Furniture and loss-making sub-categories.
- Explore opportunities to grow Home Office sales while maintaining profitability.
- Investigate why regional profit margins differ.
- Monitor both total profit and profit margin when evaluating performance.
